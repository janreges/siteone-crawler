---
name_cs: Malé a střední služby
name_en: Small & medium service businesses
---
Small and medium service businesses focused on a single vertical or a tight cluster of related services, serving a local or regional market. Hallmarks: a clear services/what-we-do section; a small named team or owner; a portfolio, gallery, or list of past projects; testimonials or reviews; opening hours, a booking/quote form, and one or a few physical locations or a defined service area. The site usually speaks to one buyer — a prospective client — with no investor relations, no subsidiaries, and rarely a large careers section. Belongs here: a digital or marketing agency, a software development studio, an accounting/bookkeeping firm, a law office, a car-repair garage, a residential construction or renovation company, a hair-and-beauty salon, a farm-to-table restaurant, a boutique hotel, a fitness studio or gym, an independent tradesperson (plumber, electrician), a dental clinic. NOT this type: a diversified group with divisions and shareholder pages → corporate; a single SaaS/app sold self-service with pricing plans → product; a store selling physical goods with a cart and checkout → ecommerce.

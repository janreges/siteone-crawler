---
name_cs: Osobní weby a osobní značky
name_en: Personal Websites & Personal Brands
---
Choose this type when the subject of the site is a single individual presenting themselves, their work and their personal brand — the "about", the voice and the offerings all center on one named person rather than a company or a product. This covers freelancers, independent consultants, coaches, keynote speakers, photographers, artists, musicians, writers and authors, indie software developers, academics, and personal bloggers or content creators. Examples that belong: a freelance UX designer's portfolio, an independent management consultant, a keynote speaker's site, a wedding photographer, a novelist's author site, a solo developer's personal site, a nutrition coach, a personal essay blog. NOT this type: a solo-founder company whose site is built entirely around one shippable app or SaaS → product; a small studio or agency that presents a team and a company brand even if founder-led → corporate; a creator whose site exists mainly to sell one flagship course or membership product → product. The deciding signal is that the person is the subject and the throughline, not an organization or a single productized offering.

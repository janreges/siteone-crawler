---
name_cs: Veřejná správa
name_en: Government & public administration
---
Choose this type for bodies that exercise public authority or deliver public administration: ministries, government agencies, cities and municipalities, regional governments, state institutions, and EU institutions. Signals: an official notice board (úřední deska), life-situation or e-service guidance for citizens, legislation/decrees/bylaws, budgets and open-data or contract registers, council or committee meeting records, named elected representatives and departments, and official channels such as a data box (datová schránka). Belongs here: a ministry site like Ministerstvo financí, a city hall portal like praha.eu, the tax authority (Finanční správa), a regional/kraj government, a small municipality's site, the labour office (Úřad práce), an EU institution on europa.eu, the statistical office, and a public register or supervisory authority. NOT this type: a state-owned enterprise that primarily sells to the public (railways or postal service with a ticket/parcel shop) → corporate/e-commerce; a public hospital or public university delivering one defined service → institution; a political party or a citizens' movement → other/political. The distinguishing test is exercise of public administration and official duties over commercial or single-service delivery.

name_cs: Odborný obsah a vzdělávání
name_en: Expert & educational content
---
Choose this type when the site's own product IS its published knowledge on a defined subject: an expert blog, professional or scientific journal, documentation/knowledge base, or a topic-focused educational resource. The organization behind it earns authority by publishing articles, tutorials, guides, research, or courses — not by selling unrelated services. Signals: a deep archive of articles, category/tag taxonomies, named authors with bylines, editorial or peer-review policy, a newsletter, and content organized by topic rather than by product SKU. Belongs here: Smashing Magazine, CSS-Tricks, MDN Web Docs, Krebs on Security, a peer-reviewed medical journal, an academic law blog, a nutrition-science site, a personal-finance education blog, a photography-tutorial hub, a national CERT's advisory blog. NOT this type: a SaaS company's marketing blog that exists to sell the product → corporate/product; a hospital or university as an institution → institution; an individual's personal portfolio/blog centered on their own career → personal; a general-news outlet covering many unrelated beats → media/publisher; a paid-course marketplace whose core is a transactional catalog (e.g. Udemy) → product/e-commerce.

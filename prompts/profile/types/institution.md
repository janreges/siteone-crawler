---
name_cs: Instituce (vzdělávání, zdraví a věda)
name_en: Institutions (education, health & research)
---
Choose this type for public-service and public-interest organizations whose primary purpose is to provide education, healthcare, or research rather than to sell products or to govern. Typical members include teaching and general hospitals (e.g. FN Motol), private and outpatient clinics, primary and secondary schools, grammar schools (gymnázia) and conservatories, universities and their faculties (e.g. Charles University), public and academic libraries (e.g. the National Library), and research institutes such as those of the Czech Academy of Sciences. Also included: polyclinics, rehabilitation centres, kindergartens, and vocational schools. The website usually centres on patients, students, readers, or the scholarly community; it describes departments, study fields, clinical specialisms, collections, admissions, and research output, and it rarely emphasises pricing or e-commerce. NOT this type: a ministry, city hall, or municipal authority → government/public administration. NOT this type: a pharmaceutical or medical-device company, or a private firm selling its own online courses → corporate/product. NOT this type: a commercial e-learning marketplace aggregating other providers' courses → portal-directory.

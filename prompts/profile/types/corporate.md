---
name_cs: Velké korporace a skupiny
name_en: Large corporations & groups
---
Large corporations, enterprise groups, and holding companies with a broad, multi-vertical footprint. Hallmarks: several distinct business divisions or segments; a portfolio of subsidiaries, brands, or member companies; a dedicated careers/jobs section with many openings; investor-relations or "for shareholders" pages, annual reports, and financial results; a press/media newsroom; corporate-governance, board, and executive-leadership pages; and often sustainability/ESG or CSR reporting. The site speaks to several audiences at once — customers, investors, job-seekers, journalists, partners. Belongs here: E.ON, Microsoft, ČEZ, Škoda Auto, Siemens, Nestlé, Deutsche Telekom, Allianz, Vodafone, a national retail bank, a regional insurer, a listed telco, an energy utility, a diversified holding group. NOT this type: a single SaaS or app with pricing tiers and feature pages → product; an online store built around a product catalog and shopping cart → ecommerce; a local single-vertical service firm such as a law office or car-repair shop → smb-services. When in doubt, the co-presence of subsidiaries/segments plus investor relations is the strongest signal for corporate.

name_cs: Produkt / SaaS / aplikace
name_en: Single product / SaaS / app
---
Websites built to market and sell a single product — a SaaS app, mobile or desktop application, developer tool/API, browser extension, video game, or a hardware device. Hallmarks: a product-centric landing page with a hero and a primary call to action (sign up, download, buy, start free trial); dedicated features/capabilities pages; a pricing page with plans or a one-time price; use-case or solution pages; and often an integrations/marketplace page, documentation, a changelog or release notes, and a roadmap. The narrative is about the product itself — what it does and the problem it solves — not about a firm's many divisions. Belongs here: Slack, Figma, Notion, 1Password, Linear, a mobile fitness app, a smart-home hardware gadget, an indie video game, a Chrome extension, a developer SDK/API product, a note-taking app. NOT this type: a company marketing many products with subsidiaries and investor relations → corporate; an agency that builds custom software for clients → smb-services; an online shop reselling many third-party goods with a cart → ecommerce. A single named product with its own pricing and docs is the clearest signal.

Choose this type ONLY when none of the 12 specific types clearly fits — it is the universal bucket
for hard-to-classify subjects, and picking it is always better than forcing a poor fit elsewhere.
Typical members: hobby and passion-project sites, fan sites and fan wikis, art or experimental web
projects, mixed-purpose sites combining several unrelated roles (e.g. half personal blog, half
company presentation, half shop), umbrella sites of loose initiatives or consortia without a clear
organizational form, link hubs and landing pages whose purpose is unclear, placeholder or
under-construction sites with little content, political parties and citizens' movements that are
not clearly membership associations, and any site whose subject cannot be confidently identified
from its content. Signals: no dominant transactional, editorial, or organizational pattern; content
that spans several of the specific types without any of them prevailing; or too little content to
tell. NOT this type: if a site merely mixes a blog with a clear services offer of one firm →
smb-services; a personal blog with a clear individual as the subject → personal; a niche topic site
with a real editorial archive → expert-content. When in doubt between a specific type and general,
choose the specific type only if its core signals are actually present.

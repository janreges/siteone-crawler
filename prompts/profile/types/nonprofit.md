---
name_cs: Nezisková organizace
name_en: Nonprofit & charity
---
Choose this type for mission-driven organizations that exist to serve a cause or community rather than to generate profit: charities, foundations, associations, community initiatives, and religious congregations. Signals: language of mission, impact, and beneficiaries; calls to donate, volunteer, or become a member; annual reports and transparency pages; grant, patron, or supporter listings; a governing board or trustees; and a legal form such as association, foundation, endowment fund, or charitable trust. Belongs here: Člověk v tísni (People in Need), a local animal shelter, a scout troop, a patient-advocacy association, a parish/church congregation, a food bank, an environmental NGO like Hnutí DUHA, a scholarship-granting foundation, a hospice charity, a community sports club. NOT this type: a public hospital or a university, which delivers a defined public service as an institution → institution; a ministry, agency, or municipality exercising public authority → government; a mission-branded company that primarily sells goods or services (a social enterprise or B-corp with a storefront) → corporate/e-commerce. When an entity both serves a cause and sells extensively, weigh whether donations/volunteering/mission or a commercial catalog dominates the site.

Choose this type when the site's primary purpose is selling physical or digital goods directly through an online storefront: it has a product catalog, browsable categories, individual product pages with prices and an add-to-cart / checkout flow, plus shipping, payment and returns information. This covers single-brand direct-to-consumer shops, large multi-category retailers, specialty niche stores, and multi-seller marketplaces. Examples that belong: Alza, Zalando, Amazon, Etsy, a small specialty coffee roaster's shop, a boutique fashion e-shop, an online pharmacy, a pet-supplies store, a marketplace connecting independent sellers. NOT this type: a single-product SaaS or app landing site that markets one software product with pricing tiers → product; a manufacturer or B2B company that showcases its offerings but has no online checkout → corporate; a personal maker who sells a few prints through a link-in-bio but whose site is really a portfolio → personal. The deciding signal is a genuine transactional storefront (catalog + cart + checkout + delivery/returns), not merely the presence of a "buy" or "pricing" page.

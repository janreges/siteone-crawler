---
name_cs: Zpravodajské a magazínové weby
name_en: News & Magazine Sites
---
Choose this type when the site is a news outlet or magazine with an editorial operation: it publishes a steady, high-frequency stream of articles produced by an editorial team or named journalists, organized into sections or desks (news, politics, business, culture, tech, lifestyle, sport, opinion), typically with datelines, bylines and an "about us"/masthead describing the newsroom. This covers general daily news portals, regional dailies, and specialized magazines across tech, business, lifestyle, culture and science. Examples that belong: iDNES, Deník N, The Verge, Vogue, TechCrunch, Politico, Ars Technica, a regional daily newspaper, a lifestyle or fashion magazine. NOT this type: a company blog that posts occasional marketing articles alongside its product pages → corporate; a single-author personal blog or newsletter written by one named individual → personal; a content-marketing "magazine" run by a brand mainly to promote its own products → corporate. The deciding signal is a genuine editorial newsroom publishing frequently across multiple sections, distinct from a brand's own commercial or a single person's voice.

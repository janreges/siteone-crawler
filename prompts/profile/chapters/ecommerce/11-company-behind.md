## selection_focus
Look for "about us", "our story", "team", "careers", "press", and any corporate/legal pages (company details, imprint) that identify who operates the shop.

## synthesis_instructions
Identify the organization running the shop: legal/company name, history and milestones, size, ownership, headquarters, and any named founders or leadership. Include employer-brand signals from careers pages where relevant (values, scale, hiring). Write in paragraphs and list named people with their roles. Rely only on stated facts; distinguish verifiable company details (registration, addresses) from narrative storytelling. If the operator is opaque and the site says little, report just the verifiable identifiers or omit.

## selection_focus
Look for "about", "our story", "philosophy", "why us", "sustainability" or values pages, and any manifesto-style content that explains what the shop stands for and how it differentiates itself.

## synthesis_instructions
Describe how the shop positions itself in the market: its stated mission and values, the price/quality segment it targets (budget, mid-market, premium, luxury), and the differentiators it claims (curation, sustainability, expertise, speed, exclusivity, local sourcing). Write in flowing paragraphs. Separate genuine positioning stated on the site from generic marketing language, and note when a claimed differentiator is backed by concrete detail versus asserted without support. If the site provides no meaningful positioning content, produce a single short paragraph or omit the chapter.

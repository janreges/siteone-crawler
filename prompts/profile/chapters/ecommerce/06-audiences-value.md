## selection_focus
Draw on the homepage, about page, category pages framed for specific audiences (e.g. "for professionals", "gifts for him"), and any content explaining who the shop is for and why to buy there.

## synthesis_instructions
Explain who the shop is built for (demographics, use cases, buyer types such as bargain hunters, gift buyers, professionals, enthusiasts) and the core value proposition it offers each — price, selection, convenience, expertise, service, or trust. Use paragraphs, optionally with a short bullet list mapping audience segments to the benefits pitched at them. Base audience inferences on concrete signals in the content (category framing, guides, tone), not assumptions. If the site gives no clear audience signals, keep this brief and factual.

## selection_focus
Prioritize category and subcategory landing pages, the main navigation/menu, and any "departments", "shop by category", or catalog index pages. These reveal the breadth and structure of what is sold.

## synthesis_instructions
This is the heart of the profile: map the assortment as completely as the site allows. Present the top-level category tree as a structured bullet hierarchy (top categories with their notable subcategories), then add paragraphs characterizing the depth and breadth — is it a broad general store or a narrow specialist, which categories appear most developed, and any specialty or hero categories the site emphasizes. Where the site states product counts per category, include them. Use the site's own category names. Do not invent categories or products that are not shown; if the navigation is thin, describe only what is visible.

## selection_focus
Prefer the homepage, the main "about us" / "about the shop" page, and any company/story page. These pages usually state what the shop sells, its scale, and its positioning in one place.

## synthesis_instructions
Open with a concise executive summary (2–3 paragraphs) that tells the reader what this shop is, what broad categories of goods it sells, whether it is a single-brand store, a multi-category retailer, or a marketplace, and who it appears to serve. Follow with a short "at a glance" bullet list of the most concrete facts stated on the site (year founded, markets/countries served, number of products or brands, store count, ownership). Keep the tone factual and neutral; do not repeat marketing slogans as if they were facts. State only what the source pages support; if a fact is not present on the site, omit it rather than guessing.

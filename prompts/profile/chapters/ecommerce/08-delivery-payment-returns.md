## selection_focus
Target "shipping", "delivery", "payment methods", "returns", "refunds", "exchanges", and FAQ or help-center pages covering fulfilment and post-purchase policies.

## synthesis_instructions
Lay out the practical purchase logistics in three clearly labelled parts: delivery (carriers, options, costs, delivery times, free-shipping thresholds, pickup points, geographic coverage), payment (accepted methods, installments, cash-on-delivery), and returns (return window, conditions, refund/exchange process, who pays return shipping). A short table or grouped bullet lists work best for the concrete terms. Keep it strictly factual and quote the site's stated terms. If any of the three areas is not documented on the site, cover the ones that are and note the absence plainly rather than inventing terms.

## selection_focus
Look for "sale", "deals", "outlet", "clearance", coupon/voucher pages, price-match or price-guarantee pages, and any pricing or financing information.

## synthesis_instructions
Describe the shop's pricing and promotional mechanics as evidenced on the site: sale/outlet sections, recurring campaigns, coupon or voucher systems, bundle deals, price-match guarantees, financing or installment options, and any free-shipping thresholds. Use a bullet list for the distinct mechanisms and short paragraphs for how aggressively price is used as a lever. Report only mechanisms actually present; do not quote specific transient prices as permanent facts. Omit gracefully if the site shows no promotional or pricing-policy content.

## selection_focus
Look for on-site review/rating summaries, testimonials, "as seen in" or press mentions, trust badges, certifications, awards, and any independent-review-platform integrations.

## synthesis_instructions
Compile the credibility and trust signals the shop presents: aggregate ratings, customer testimonials, third-party review-platform scores, press mentions, awards, certifications, and trust seals. Use a bullet list of the distinct proof points, with a short interpretive paragraph on how strongly the shop leans on social proof. Attribute claims to their source and do not treat marketing testimonials as independent verification. Omit gracefully if the site presents no social proof.

## selection_focus
Look for a "brands", "shop by brand", "our partners", or brand index page, and brand filters within categories. Multi-brand retailers usually list the brands they stock.

## synthesis_instructions
List the brands, manufacturers, or suppliers the shop carries, grouped by category or tier (e.g. premium vs everyday) where the site makes that distinction clear. Use a bulleted or grouped list rather than prose when there are many names. Note any exclusive, own-label, or flagship brand relationships the site highlights. Report only brands actually named on the site. If the shop is single-brand or lists no brands (e.g. a direct-to-consumer or handmade store), omit this chapter gracefully.

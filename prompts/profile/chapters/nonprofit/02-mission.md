## selection_focus
"Mission", "Vision", "Values", "Our cause", "What we stand for", and charter/manifesto or statute-summary pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Articulate the organization's mission (the change it seeks), its vision (the world it works toward), and its stated values or guiding principles. Use short paragraphs, adding a bullet list only where the site explicitly enumerates values or strategic goals. Distinguish mission from concrete programs, which belong in the next chapter, to prevent duplication. Ground every statement in the site's own wording and do not embellish the mission beyond what is written. If the site states no mission or values, omit this chapter with a single sentence noting the absence.

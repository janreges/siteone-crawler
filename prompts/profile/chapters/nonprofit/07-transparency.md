## selection_focus
"Annual report", "Financial statements", "Transparency", "How we use funds", "Where the money goes", and grant/funding-source pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Report what the organization discloses about its finances and accountability: annual reports, income and expenditure, the split between programs and overhead, funding sources, and audit or oversight statements. Present available figures in a compact table or bullet list (year, income, expenditure, program share) and link the reader to the reports the site publishes. Use only numbers and documents actually shown, cite the reporting year, and never estimate or reconstruct financials. Note plainly if reports exist but details are only in downloadable documents. If the site provides no financial or transparency information, omit this chapter with a brief note.

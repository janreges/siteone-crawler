## selection_focus
"Impact", "Results", "What we've achieved", "Success stories", "Case studies", testimonials, and outcome or statistics pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Summarize the results and outcomes the organization reports: quantitative impact figures, milestones reached, and qualitative success stories or testimonials. Lead with any concrete numbers in a short bullet list or small table (metric and value), then narrate one or two representative success stories the site tells. Attribute all figures and stories to the site and never fabricate or round up impact numbers; present testimonials as quoted claims, not verified fact. Keep language measured despite the celebratory nature of source material. If the site reports no outcomes, metrics, or stories, omit this chapter with a brief note.

## selection_focus
"Donate", "Support us", "Give", "Volunteer", "Get involved", "Become a member", "Membership", and campaign or fundraising-appeal pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Lay out every way a reader can support the organization: donating (methods, one-off vs recurring, bank details, tax-deductibility), volunteering (roles, how to sign up), membership (tiers and benefits), and in-kind or other support. Use clearly labeled subsections or a bullet list per support type so the reader can act. Include concrete, quoted details such as account numbers, donation URLs, or membership fees exactly as published. Keep it practical and factual rather than persuasive. If the site offers no way to donate, volunteer, or join, omit this chapter with one sentence.

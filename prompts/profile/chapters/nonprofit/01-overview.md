## selection_focus
The homepage, "About us", and any "Who we are"/"Our story" landing page summarizing the organization and its cause.

## synthesis_instructions
Write a concise two-to-three paragraph overview conveying what the organization is, the cause it serves, whom it helps, its legal form and scale if evident, and how active it appears. Give the reader the essence quickly; reserve depth for later chapters. Use plain prose without headings or bullets. Keep the tone factual and restrained — describe the work, avoid emotive fundraising language, and do not assert impact figures not shown on the pages. State only what the selected pages support, and if they do not describe the organization's purpose, note that in one sentence and stop.

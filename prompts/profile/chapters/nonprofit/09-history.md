## selection_focus

## synthesis_instructions
Recount the organization's origins and development: when and why it was founded, by whom, and the key milestones or turning points in its growth. A chronological timeline (bullet list of year and event) works well, optionally preceded by a short narrative paragraph. Use only dates and events the site states, and do not fill gaps with assumed history. Keep the narrative factual rather than mythologizing the founding. If the site provides no history or founding information, omit this chapter with a brief note.

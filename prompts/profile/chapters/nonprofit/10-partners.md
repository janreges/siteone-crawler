## selection_focus

## synthesis_instructions
List the organizations that support the nonprofit's work: corporate partners, institutional donors, grant-makers, and collaborating organizations, grouped by the role the site assigns them. Present as a bullet list or table of partner name and relationship, with a short note where the site describes the partnership. Include only partners the site actually names and do not infer relationships from logos without context beyond stating they appear. Keep it factual, avoiding promotional framing of the partnerships. If no partners or supporters are named on the site, omit this chapter with one sentence.

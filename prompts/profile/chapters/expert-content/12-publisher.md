## selection_focus
"About us", "Company", "Imprint"/legal notice, "Team", "Contact", and footer pages naming the owning organization or legal entity.

## synthesis_instructions
Identify the organization or entity that owns and operates the publication: its legal name, form, location, founding, and relationship to any parent company or institution, plus the operating team if distinct from the bylined authors. Write as short factual paragraphs; a small facts table (entity, founded, location, parent) is welcome if the data exists. Report only ownership and organizational facts the site states, distinguishing the publisher from individual authors covered earlier. Do not infer corporate structure the pages do not show. If the site never identifies an operating organization, omit this chapter with one sentence noting the anonymity.

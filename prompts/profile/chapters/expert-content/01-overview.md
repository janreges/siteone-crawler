## selection_focus
The homepage, an "About"/"About this site" page, and any top-level landing or "start here" page that explains what the publication is and what it covers.

## synthesis_instructions
Write a tight two-to-three paragraph executive summary that lets a reader grasp in under a minute what this publication is, the single subject or field it authoritatively covers, who runs it, and roughly how established and active it appears (archive depth, publishing cadence) if the pages reveal it. Cover the "what and for whom" at a high level; leave detailed treatment to later chapters. Use plain declarative prose, no headings or bullets. Keep the tone factual and neutral — describe, do not promote, and never call the site "leading" or "best" unless quoting the source. State only what the selected pages support, and if they do not describe the publication's purpose at all, write a single sentence noting that and stop.

## selection_focus
Category, tag, section, or "topics" index pages, the main navigation's content taxonomy, and hub/pillar pages that group articles by theme.

## synthesis_instructions
Map the subject areas the publication covers, ideally reconstructing its own taxonomy of categories, tags, or sections and noting which themes appear most developed or emphasized. Prefer a structured layout: a short framing paragraph followed by a bullet list or a two-column table of topic areas with a one-line description of each, drawn from category pages or hub descriptions. Where the source shows relative depth (e.g. a category with far more articles), note it factually. Do not invent topics that are not represented in the navigation or category structure. If the site has no discernible topic taxonomy, describe the subject matter in a single paragraph or omit the chapter with a brief note.

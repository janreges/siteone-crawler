## selection_focus
Author/contributor index pages, individual author bio pages, "Our team"/"Editorial board" pages, and bylines linking to author profiles.

## synthesis_instructions
Profile the people who create the content: named authors, contributors, and any editorial board or expert reviewers, with the credentials, roles, or areas of expertise the site states for each. Prefer a table or bullet list with name, role/title, and a short credential or focus note per person. Report only verifiable details from author pages or bios — real names, stated qualifications, affiliations — and never fabricate credentials or invent contributors. Where the site is authored anonymously or by an unnamed team, state that plainly. If no author or contributor information is present anywhere, omit this chapter with one sentence.

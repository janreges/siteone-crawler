Organize as a few short paragraphs; use a compact bullet list only if the source enumerates explicit principles or scope statements. Distinguish mission (why they publish) from topic breadth (covered in the next chapter) to avoid overlap. Keep it factual and grounded in the site's wording; do not infer a mission the pages never state.

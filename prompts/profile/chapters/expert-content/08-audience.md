## selection_focus
"About", "Who this is for", audience or membership pages, "advertise with us"/media-kit pages, and difficulty/level indicators on content.

## synthesis_instructions
Characterize the intended readership: the profession, expertise level (beginner to expert/practitioner), and interests the content targets, plus any audience descriptions the site or its media kit provides. Write as a short paragraph or two; use bullets only if the site lists distinct reader segments. Base claims on stated audience language, level tags, or media-kit descriptions rather than guessing from tone. Do not conflate this with the mission chapter — focus specifically on who reads, not why the site publishes. If the pages give no indication of the intended audience, omit the chapter or cover it in one factual sentence.

## selection_focus
Pages or sections for tutorials, guides, videos, podcasts, courses, newsletters, whitepapers, case studies, cheat sheets, or named recurring series/columns.

## synthesis_instructions
Catalog the forms the content takes beyond plain articles — for example tutorials, long-form guides, video, podcasts, courses, downloadable resources, interactive tools, or named recurring series. Present as a bullet list where each item names the format and briefly says what it offers and how it is used, or as short paragraphs if formats are few. Note any signature series or columns by name if the site brands them. Keep descriptions concrete and sourced from the pages; avoid speculation about production quality. If the publication offers only standard articles with no distinct formats or series, say so in one sentence and omit the rest.

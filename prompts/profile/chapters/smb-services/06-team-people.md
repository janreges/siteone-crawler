## selection_focus
Target "Team", "Our team", "Staff", "Meet the team", and owner/founder or individual-bio pages, usually under /about/team or /team.

## synthesis_instructions
Introduce the people behind the business: owner(s), key staff, and specialists, with their roles and any stated qualifications or experience. A bulleted or tabular roster (Name / Role / Note) plus a short framing sentence works well. Keep names, titles, and credentials verbatim and do not infer roles. A line or two per person is enough. Include only individuals the site names and omit the chapter gracefully if no team or people information is published.

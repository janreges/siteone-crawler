## selection_focus
Prefer the homepage and a top-level "About" / "About us" page. On small sites the homepage alone usually carries the whole pitch.

## synthesis_instructions
Open with one tight paragraph naming the business, its trade or vertical, and where it operates, then 1-3 short paragraphs summarizing what it does, who it helps, and what makes it distinctive as the site frames it. This is the reader's quick orientation, so use flowing prose, not lists, and avoid duplicating the detailed services chapter. Keep it factual and resist repeating marketing superlatives in your own voice. State only what the pages support and keep it short if the site is sparse; omit the chapter only if there is no descriptive content at all.

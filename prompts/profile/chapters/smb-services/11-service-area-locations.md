## selection_focus
Look for "Service area", "Areas we cover", "Locations", "Where we work", branch pages, and maps, usually on /contact or /about.

## synthesis_instructions
Describe where the business operates: its physical location(s), the geographic area or towns/regions it serves, and any travel or coverage limits. A short paragraph plus a bullet list of covered areas or branches works well. Report place names and any radius/coverage statements exactly as written and do not extrapolate coverage. Keep it factual. Omit the chapter gracefully if the site gives no location or service-area information.

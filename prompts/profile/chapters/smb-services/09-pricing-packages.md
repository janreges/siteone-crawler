## selection_focus
Prefer "Pricing", "Prices", "Packages", "Rates", "Menu" (for restaurants), "Plans", and "Get a quote" pages, plus any price lists on services pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Report how the business prices its work: published prices, packages or tiers, hourly/day rates, menus, or a quote-on-request model. Use a table (Item or package / Price / Includes) when prices are listed, otherwise describe the pricing approach in prose. Copy all figures, currencies, and units exactly and never estimate a price the site does not state. If pricing is quote-only, say so plainly and note how a quote is obtained. Include only stated pricing and omit the chapter gracefully if the site publishes no pricing information.

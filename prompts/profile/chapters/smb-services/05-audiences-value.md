## selection_focus

## synthesis_instructions
Describe the customers the business targets and the value it promises them: the types of clients served, the problems it solves, and the benefits or guarantees it emphasizes. Use short paragraphs by audience or a bullet list of key benefits. Draw on how the site frames its ideal client and differentiators rather than inferring from services alone. Keep the tone analytical and avoid restating slogans as fact. Include only what the pages support and omit the chapter if the site names no clear audience or value proposition.

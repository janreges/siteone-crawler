## selection_focus
Select every services page: the "Services" / "What we do" hub and each individual service page (e.g. /services/*, and named offerings like "Roof repair", "Tax returns", "Web design"). This is the core chapter — prefer breadth across all service pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Give a thorough, structured account of the services the business offers, one subsection or a table row per service (Service / What it includes / Good to know). For each, describe what it covers, typical deliverables or scope, and any specialization or constraint the page states. Group related services under headings where the site does. Keep descriptions concrete and functional, avoiding sales adjectives. Do not invent services or scope beyond the pages; if a service is only named without detail, list it plainly. Omit the chapter only if the site describes no services at all (unusual for this type).

## selection_focus
Look for "About", "Our story", "Who we are", "Why us", and founder/owner background pages, usually under /about.

## synthesis_instructions
Tell the business's story: when and why it was founded, its background and experience, its mission or philosophy, and what it says sets it apart. Use short narrative paragraphs; a compact bullet list works for an explicit set of values or differentiators. Preserve founding years and any stated experience ("15 years in the trade") exactly. Keep the tone factual and attribute self-descriptions to the business. Include only what the pages support and omit the chapter gracefully if the site offers no background or story.

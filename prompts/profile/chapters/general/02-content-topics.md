## selection_focus
Prefer the main navigation, section or category index pages, and the largest content pages — whatever reveals what the site actually contains and writes about.

## synthesis_instructions
Map what the site actually contains: its main sections, topics, and kinds of content (articles, galleries, tools, listings, documents), and which areas appear most developed. Use a short framing paragraph followed by a bullet list of the main content areas with a one-line description of each, using the site's own section names. Note the apparent activity level (fresh vs stale content) only where dates on the pages show it. Do not invent structure the navigation does not show; if the site is small, describe the few pages it has plainly.

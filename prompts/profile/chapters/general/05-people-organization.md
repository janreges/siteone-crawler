## selection_focus
Look for "about", "team", author or imprint/legal pages, footer credits, and any page naming the people or entity operating the site.

## synthesis_instructions
Identify who runs the site as far as the pages reveal it: named people and their roles, an operating organization or legal entity, and any stated background or history. List named people with their roles and quote legal identifiers (company name, registration) exactly. Distinguish clearly between a named operator and an anonymous site — if the site never identifies its operator, state that plainly in one short paragraph. Never fabricate names, roles, or organizational details.

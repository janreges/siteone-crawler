## selection_focus
Look for any pages describing services, products, downloads, tools, memberships, or other things a visitor can get, buy, join, or use.

## synthesis_instructions

Use a bullet list or short subsections per offering, quoting prices and terms exactly as published. Keep descriptions functional and free of promotional language. Report only offerings the pages actually describe; if the site offers nothing (a purely informational or artistic site), return an empty result so the chapter is omitted.

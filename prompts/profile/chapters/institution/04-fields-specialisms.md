## selection_focus
Look for pages describing medical specialties, academic disciplines, study areas, research fields, or collection domains — often under "Specialisms", "Fields of study", "Research areas", "What we do", or individual department pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Detail the substantive fields the institution works in — clinical specialties for a hospital, academic disciplines and study fields for a school or university, subject domains for a library or research institute. Group related fields and use bullet lists or subheadings so a reader can quickly scan the breadth and depth of expertise. For each field, add a sentence of context where the source pages provide it (what it covers, who leads it, notable strengths). Keep it descriptive and factual, not promotional. Draw only on what the pages state; where coverage is uneven, describe the well-documented fields and note briefly that others are listed without detail. Omit the chapter if the site does not describe any fields or specialisms.

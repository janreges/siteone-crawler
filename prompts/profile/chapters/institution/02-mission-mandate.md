## selection_focus
Look for "Mission", "Vision", "Values", "Strategy", charter, founding-purpose, ethics, or annual-report/"about" pages that state why the institution exists and the principles guiding it.

## synthesis_instructions
Describe the institution's mission, its public-service mandate or statutory purpose, and the core values or guiding principles it states. Where the site distinguishes a vision from a mission, or lists explicit values, present them as short labelled paragraphs or a bullet list. Note any code of ethics, quality charter, or strategic goals the pages describe. Keep the tone factual and quote distinctive mission wording sparingly and verbatim rather than paraphrasing it into marketing language. Cover only what the source pages state and omit this chapter gracefully if the site expresses no explicit mission, mandate, or values.

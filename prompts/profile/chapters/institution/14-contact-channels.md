## selection_focus
Look for "Contact", "Contacts", the footer, "Reach us", department contact pages, and social-media or newsletter links.

## synthesis_instructions
List the institution's contact points and communication channels: main and departmental phone numbers, e-mail addresses, postal address, contact form, emergency or helpline numbers, social-media profiles, and any newsletter or app. Present them as a clearly labelled bullet list or table (channel — detail). Reproduce phone numbers, e-mails, and handles exactly as written and never invent contact details. Keep it purely factual with no narrative. Include only channels the pages provide, and omit the chapter if the site lists no contact information.

## selection_focus
Prefer "Visit us", "Opening hours", "How to get here", "Directions", "Parking", "Accessibility", "Visitor information", or patient/visitor practical-info pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Gather the practical information a visitor, patient, or student needs: opening or visiting hours, address and directions, parking and public transport, accessibility provisions, and any house rules or on-site amenities. Present hours and locations in a compact table or bullet list and directions in short paragraphs. Include only details the pages state, and give exact hours, addresses, and rules verbatim. Keep the tone practical and neutral. Omit the chapter gracefully if the site provides no practical visitor information.

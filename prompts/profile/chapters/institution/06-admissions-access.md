## synthesis_instructions
Explain how a prospective student, patient, reader, or member gains access: application and admission procedures, entrance requirements and deadlines for schools and universities; referral, registration or appointment processes for clinics and hospitals; membership or registration steps for a library. Present multi-step processes as numbered steps and requirements as bullet lists. Include eligibility criteria, required documents, key dates, and any fees the pages state. Keep the tone procedural and factual. Report only what the pages specify and, where the process is only partly documented, say so plainly. Omit the chapter if the site describes no admission, enrolment, or access process.

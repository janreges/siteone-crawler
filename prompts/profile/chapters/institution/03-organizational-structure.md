## selection_focus
Prefer "Departments", "Faculties", "Clinics", "Wards", "Institutes", "Divisions", "Organisation", "Structure", "Management", or site-map pages that enumerate the institution's constituent units.

## synthesis_instructions
Map the institution's internal structure: its faculties, departments, clinics, wards, institutes, centres, branches, or divisions, and how they relate. Prefer a structured bullet list or a table (unit name — focus/role) when the site enumerates many units; use short paragraphs when structure is described narratively. Include governing or administrative bodies (board, rectorate, directorate, senate) if the pages name them. Do not conflate this with the fields-of-expertise or services chapters — here the emphasis is the organisational anatomy, not the subject matter or the client-facing offering. State only units the pages actually name, and omit the chapter if the site does not describe any internal structure.

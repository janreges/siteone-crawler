## selection_focus
Prefer "Management", "Leadership", "Our team", "Staff", "Faculty", "Doctors", "Professors", "Rector", "Director", or profile pages naming individuals and their roles or expertise.

## synthesis_instructions
Present the institution's leadership and notable staff: directors, rectors, deans, chief physicians, heads of department, professors, or lead researchers, with their roles and stated areas of expertise. Use a bullet list or a table (name — role — focus) when many individuals are named, and short paragraphs for a small leadership team. Include credentials, titles, or specialisms only as the pages state them. Do not fabricate names, titles, or biographies. Cover only individuals the pages actually name, and omit the chapter if the site does not identify any people.

## selection_focus

## synthesis_instructions
Describe the concrete, client-facing services the institution provides: patient care pathways and outpatient/inpatient services for a hospital; teaching, tutoring, counselling, careers and welfare support for a school or university; lending, reference, digitisation, study spaces and events for a library. Organise by user group or service category using subheadings and bullet lists. Distinguish this from the fields chapter by focusing on what a user actually receives and how they use it, not the underlying discipline. Maintain a factual, service-catalogue tone. Include only services the pages describe, and omit the chapter gracefully if the site does not present user-facing services.

## selection_focus
Prefer the homepage, the main "About" / "About us" page, and any "Who we are", "Introduction", or annual-report landing page that describes the institution as a whole.

## synthesis_instructions
Open with a concise executive summary (2-4 paragraphs) identifying the institution, its type (hospital, university, school, library, research institute), its size and reach, and the public it serves. Follow with what it does at the highest level and why it exists, without repeating the detailed mission wording covered later. Write in neutral, factual prose; avoid promotional adjectives and superlatives that the source pages do not substantiate. State only facts the crawled pages support, and if the pages are too thin to characterise the institution, produce a short honest summary rather than inventing detail. Omit the chapter only if no page describes the institution at all.

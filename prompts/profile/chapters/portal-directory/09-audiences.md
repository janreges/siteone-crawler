## selection_focus
Prefer "About", "How it works", segment landing pages, and any content that identifies the consumers and the providers the platform targets.

## synthesis_instructions
Characterise the two-sided audience: the consumers the platform serves (their needs and segments) and the providers it recruits (their type and size). Use short paragraphs or a two-column framing (users vs providers) describing each side. Ground every audience claim in wording or targeting the pages actually show, rather than inferring a demographic the site never states. Keep the tone analytical and factual. Include only what the pages support, and omit the chapter if the site does not indicate who it is for.

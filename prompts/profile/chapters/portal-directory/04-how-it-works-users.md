## selection_focus
Look for "How it works", "For buyers", "For candidates", "For travellers", "Help", or guide pages describing search, comparison, booking, or purchase flows from the consumer's side.

## synthesis_instructions
Explain the consumer journey step by step: how users search, filter, and browse; how they compare options; and how they enquire, apply, book, or buy. Present the main flow as numbered steps and supporting features as bullet lists. Cover account creation, saved searches or alerts, and what happens after a user acts (redirect to a provider, on-platform checkout, application submission) where the pages describe it. Keep the tone factual and procedural. Describe only the flows the pages document, and omit the chapter if the site does not explain the user experience.

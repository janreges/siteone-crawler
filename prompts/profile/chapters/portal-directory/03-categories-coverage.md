## selection_focus

## synthesis_instructions
Map the platform's breadth: its main categories and subcategories, verticals, and geographic or language coverage. Present the taxonomy as a structured bullet list or table (category — what it covers) and describe geographic reach in short prose. Convey the shape and depth of coverage without reproducing an exhaustive category tree — summarise the top levels and note the depth beneath. Keep the tone factual. Draw only on categories and regions the pages actually list, and omit the chapter gracefully if the site presents no category or coverage structure.

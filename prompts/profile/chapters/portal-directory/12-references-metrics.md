## selection_focus
Look for "About", press, "In numbers", "Statistics", testimonials, case-study, and awards pages containing usage figures, endorsements, or recognition.

## synthesis_instructions
Gather the platform's proof points: audience and usage metrics (visitors, listings, transactions), testimonials from users or providers, case studies, media mentions, and awards. Present quantitative figures in a compact list or table and quote testimonials briefly and verbatim with attribution where given. Reproduce all figures exactly and never invent or round numbers the pages do not state. Keep the tone factual and restrained. Include only what the pages show, and omit the chapter gracefully if the site presents no metrics or references.

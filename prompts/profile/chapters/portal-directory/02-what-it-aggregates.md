## selection_focus
Look for pages describing the core inventory — products, listings, jobs, businesses, properties, deals, or events — and any pages stating catalogue scale, sources, or "browse all" entry points.

## synthesis_instructions
Describe precisely what the platform brings together: the type of items listed (products, job vacancies, businesses, properties, offers, events), where they come from (merchants, employers, agencies, private sellers), and the scale of the inventory if stated (number of listings, merchants, categories). Use short paragraphs for the narrative and bullet lists for inventory types and sources. Distinguish clearly between what the platform aggregates and what individual providers actually sell — the platform brokers, it does not own the inventory. Keep the tone factual and use exact figures the pages state rather than rounding or embellishing. Include only what the pages document, and omit the chapter if the site does not describe its inventory.

## selection_focus
Prefer the homepage, the main "About" / "About us" page, and any "How it works" or "What is <platform>" landing page that describes the platform as a whole.

## synthesis_instructions
Open with an executive summary identifying the platform, what it aggregates, the market or vertical it serves, and the two sides it connects (consumers and providers). Explain in 2-4 paragraphs the core value proposition — why users come to it and why providers list on it. Write in neutral, factual prose and avoid the marketing superlatives common on such sites unless the pages substantiate them. State only what the crawled pages support. Omit the chapter only if no page describes the platform overall.

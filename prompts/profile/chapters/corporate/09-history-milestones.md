## selection_focus

## synthesis_instructions
Recount the organization's history: founding, key milestones, mergers/acquisitions, major expansions, and turning points, in chronological order. A timeline-style bulleted list (year — event) is ideal; add a short narrative paragraph for context. Use only dates and events the site states and preserve them verbatim; never fill gaps with assumed history. Keep the tone factual. Omit the chapter gracefully if the site provides no historical or milestone information.

## selection_focus
Look for "Where we operate", "Markets", "Locations", "Global presence", country/region pages, office directories, and maps, often under /about, /contact, or a country selector.

## synthesis_instructions
Summarize the geographic and market footprint: countries, regions, or markets served, number and location of offices or facilities, and any stated market positions (e.g. "operating in 12 countries"). A short paragraph plus a bulleted list of regions/locations works best; use a table only if the site provides structured location data. Report figures exactly as stated and do not extrapolate. Keep it factual and omit the chapter gracefully if the site gives no meaningful information about where the organization operates.

## selection_focus
Target section-hub pages for each line of business or division — e.g. /business, /segments, /divisions, /what-we-do, and the top entries of the main navigation (Energy, Mobility, Banking, Cloud, …). Prefer overview/hub pages over deep leaf pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Describe each distinct business segment, division, or vertical the group operates: what it does, who it serves, and any flagship offerings or brands within it. Structure it as one short subsection (heading plus a paragraph or two) per segment, or a table with columns Segment / What it covers / Notes when the site presents them uniformly. Preserve the company's own segment names and boundaries rather than inventing your own taxonomy. Be factual and avoid promotional phrasing. If the organization is effectively single-segment, say so briefly instead of manufacturing divisions, and omit the chapter if the site does not break the business into segments.

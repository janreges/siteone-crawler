## selection_focus
Select the products and services hub pages and their main sub-pages — /products, /services, /solutions, /offerings and category landing pages. Sample a few representative deep pages when the catalog is large, but prefer overview pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Give a structured tour of what the organization actually sells or provides, grouped by product family or service line. Use subsections or a table (Offering / Description / For whom) and keep each entry to a few factual sentences. Where offerings tie to segments or subsidiaries covered earlier, note the link without repeating those chapters. Distinguish products (things) from services (engagements) if the site does. Avoid marketing adjectives; describe function and audience. Include only offerings the pages actually document, and if the crawl surfaced a mass catalog, summarize its scope and categories rather than enumerating every item. Omit the chapter if no offering pages exist.

## selection_focus
Prefer "Careers", "Jobs", "Work with us", "Life at [company]", "Culture", "Benefits", and early-careers/graduate pages under /careers or /jobs. The open-positions listing itself is a strong signal.

## synthesis_instructions
Summarize the organization as an employer: how it describes its workplace culture and values, employee benefits and development, the scope of hiring (departments, locations, seniority), and any employer-brand or diversity messaging. Use short thematic paragraphs plus a bullet list of benefit categories. If a jobs listing is present, characterize the volume and types of roles rather than listing every vacancy. Keep the tone factual and treat culture claims as the company's own framing. Include only what the careers pages support and omit the chapter if the site has no employer or careers content.

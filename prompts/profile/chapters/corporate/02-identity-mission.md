## selection_focus
Look for "Mission", "Vision", "Our values", "Purpose", "Philosophy", "Strategy", and manifesto-style or code-of-conduct pages, usually under /about, /company, or /who-we-are.

## synthesis_instructions
Cover the organization's stated mission, vision, purpose, core values, and any strategic pillars or guiding principles. Use short thematic paragraphs; a compact bullet list is acceptable for an explicit values set. Where the site articulates a strategy or long-term ambition, summarize it faithfully. Keep the tone descriptive and avoid turning aspirational language into fact — attribute claims to the company ("the company states…"). Quote a short mission statement verbatim when one exists. Include only what the pages support and omit the chapter gracefully if the site expresses no mission, values, or strategy.

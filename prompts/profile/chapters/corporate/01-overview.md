## selection_focus
Prefer the homepage and top-level "About us" / "Company" / "Who we are" pages, plus any "At a glance", "Company profile", or fact-sheet page. These carry the highest-level framing of the whole group.

## synthesis_instructions
Open with one tight paragraph naming the organization, its industry, and what it does, then 2-4 paragraphs summarizing the breadth of the group: main business areas, scale (markets, employees, brands) as stated, and overall positioning. This is the reader's 60-second orientation, so favor flowing prose over lists and do not itemize every division (later chapters do that). Keep it factual and neutral: report claims as the site frames them but avoid amplifying superlatives ("world-leading", "best-in-class") into your own voice. State only what the source pages support; if the site gives little corporate context, keep it short rather than padding. Omit the chapter entirely if no page describes the organization as a whole.

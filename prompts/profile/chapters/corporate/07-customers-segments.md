## selection_focus
Prefer "For business / For individuals / For partners" audience pages, industry or sector pages ("Solutions for healthcare/retail/…"), and segment landing pages that describe who the offering is for.

## synthesis_instructions
Describe the audiences and customer segments the organization targets — consumer vs business, industries or sectors served, company sizes, partner and reseller channels — and the value proposition offered to each. Organize by audience or industry using short subsections or a bullet list. Draw on how the site frames "who it's for" rather than guessing from products alone. Keep the tone analytical and neutral. State only what the pages support and omit the chapter if the site does not identify distinct audiences or segments.

## selection_focus
Look for "experience", "resume/CV", "career", "clients", "roles", or a timeline of past positions and engagements.

## synthesis_instructions
Lay out the person's professional track record: significant roles, employers, clients, and career milestones, in reverse-chronological order where possible. Use a bullet list or timeline with role, organization, and dates as stated. Distinguish documented positions from broad claims of impact. If experience overlaps heavily with the biography chapter, focus here on the concrete role/client record rather than narrative. Omit if not documented.

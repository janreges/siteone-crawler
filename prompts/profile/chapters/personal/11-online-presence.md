## selection_focus
Look for social-media links (usually in the header/footer), newsletter sign-ups, community/membership links (Discord, Patreon, Substack), and "follow me" sections.

## synthesis_instructions
Map the person's presence beyond the website: social-media profiles, newsletter, podcast, YouTube/video channels, and any communities or memberships they run. Present as a compact labelled bullet list of channels, noting the primary platforms they emphasize. List only channels actually linked from the site. Keep it factual with no engagement-metric speculation.

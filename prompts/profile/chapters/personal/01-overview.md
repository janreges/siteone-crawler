## selection_focus
Prefer the homepage, the "about" / "about me" page, and any short bio or intro page. These usually state who the person is, what they do, and what they are known for.

## synthesis_instructions
Open with a 2–3 paragraph executive summary of who this person is, their profession or role, what they are best known for, and what the site is primarily for (hiring them, reading their work, booking a talk). Add a short "at a glance" bullet list of the most concrete facts stated (location, profession, years of experience, notable affiliations). Keep it factual and avoid restating self-promotional adjectives as facts. State only what the source pages support; omit any fact not present.

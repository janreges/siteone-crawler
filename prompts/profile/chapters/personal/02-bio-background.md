## selection_focus
Look for the full "about", "bio", "my story", "journey", or "background" pages, plus any longer-form personal narrative.

## synthesis_instructions
Tell the person's story as the site presents it: origins, education, career path, formative experiences, and how they arrived at their current focus. Write in flowing narrative paragraphs, in chronological order where the material allows. Draw a clear line between documented facts (degrees, past roles, dates) and self-described narrative. If the site offers only a thin bio, keep this proportionate rather than embellishing, and omit if no biographical content exists.

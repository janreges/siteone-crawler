## selection_focus
Draw on the homepage, about page, "what I do", "services", "skills", or specialty pages that describe the person's domains and competencies.

## synthesis_instructions
Summarize the person's core areas of expertise, specialisms, methods, and the topics they are authoritative on. Use a bullet list for distinct competency areas plus short paragraphs on their approach or philosophy of work. Ground each claimed area in supporting evidence on the site (services offered, work shown, topics written about) rather than listing self-declared skills alone. If expertise is not clearly articulated, keep this brief.

## selection_focus
The homepage, "About the authority/office", and any introductory page describing the institution and its role.

## synthesis_instructions
Write a concise two-to-three paragraph overview stating what the body is (its type and level of government), the territory or domain it governs, its core responsibility, and its place in the wider administrative structure if evident. Give the reader orientation quickly; leave detail to later chapters. Use plain prose, no bullets or headings. Keep the tone neutral and administrative — describe function, avoid any promotional framing of the authority. State only what the selected pages support, and if they do not describe the body's role, note that in one sentence and stop.

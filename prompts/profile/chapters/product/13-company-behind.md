## selection_focus
Prefer "About", "Company", "Team", "Our story", and "Careers" links that describe the maker of the product, usually a smaller "About" area distinct from the product pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Describe the organization that makes the product: who they are, founding/background, mission, team or founders, funding or backing if stated, and whether the product is part of a larger portfolio. Use short paragraphs plus a brief people list where named. Keep names, dates, and any funding/backer details verbatim and do not infer corporate facts. This is context about the maker, kept lighter than the product chapters. Include only what the pages support and omit the chapter gracefully if the site says little about the company behind the product.

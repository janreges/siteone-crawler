## selection_focus
Look for "Why [product]", "Benefits", "vs [competitor]" comparison pages, "How it's different", and the value/benefit sections of the homepage and solution pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Articulate the product's value proposition and positioning: the core benefits it promises, the pain points it addresses, and how it differentiates from alternatives (including any comparison or "vs" pages). Use short thematic paragraphs or a benefits bullet list. Where the site compares against competitors, summarize the claimed differences factually and attribute them to the vendor. Avoid amplifying marketing superlatives into your own assertions. Include only what the pages support and omit the chapter gracefully if the site states no clear value proposition or differentiators.

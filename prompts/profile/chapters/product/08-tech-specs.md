## selection_focus
Prefer "Specifications", "System requirements", "Supported platforms", "Tech specs", and hardware/software requirement pages, plus specs sections in docs or on the product page.

## synthesis_instructions
Summarize the product's technical specifications and requirements: supported platforms and operating systems, hardware requirements, system prerequisites, capacities/limits, languages supported, and (for hardware) physical/technical specs. A table (Attribute / Value) is ideal for spec sheets. Copy all specs, versions, and numbers exactly and never infer compatibility the site does not state. Keep it purely factual. Include only what the pages support and omit the chapter gracefully if the site publishes no specifications or requirements.

## selection_focus
Prefer the homepage/landing page and any "Overview", "What is [product]", or "Product" top-level page. The hero and intro sections carry the core pitch.

## synthesis_instructions
Open with one tight paragraph stating what the product is, its category, and the problem it solves, then 1-3 short paragraphs summarizing what it does and for whom at a high level. This is the reader's quick orientation, so use flowing prose and do not enumerate every feature (a later chapter does that). Keep it factual and avoid restating hero-copy superlatives as fact. State only what the pages support and keep it concise; omit the chapter only if no page describes the product overall.

## selection_focus
Target the "Pricing" / "Plans" page and any "Compare plans", add-ons, or billing-FAQ pages. This is usually a single, information-dense page.

## synthesis_instructions
Report the product's pricing precisely: plans or tiers, their prices and billing periods, what each tier includes, free/trial options, and any add-ons or usage limits. Present tiers as a comparison table (Plan / Price / Key inclusions) whenever the site lists them; use prose for a single price or quote-only model. Copy every price, currency, period, and limit verbatim and never estimate or compute a figure the site does not state. Note explicitly if pricing is "contact sales". Include only stated pricing and omit the chapter gracefully if the site publishes no pricing.

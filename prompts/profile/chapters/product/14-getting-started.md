## selection_focus
Look for primary calls to action — "Sign up", "Download", "Start free trial", "Buy", "Book a demo", "Contact sales" — plus the "Contact" page and footer social/support links.

## synthesis_instructions
Give the reader the practical next steps: how to obtain or try the product (sign up, download, free trial, purchase, demo request), the primary calls to action, and how to contact sales or support, including official social profiles. Present as a short bulleted list (Action / How) plus any contact details verbatim. Do not fabricate links, prices, or steps. Keep prose minimal and action-oriented. Omit the chapter gracefully if the site offers no clear way to get started or make contact.

## selection_focus

## synthesis_instructions
Gather the practical information an attendee needs: opening or performance times, address and how to get there, parking and public transport, accessibility provisions, house rules, and on-site amenities such as catering, cloakrooms, or shops. Present hours and locations in a compact list or table and directions and rules in short paragraphs. Reproduce exact hours, addresses, and rules and include only details the pages state. Keep the tone practical and neutral. Omit the chapter if the site provides no practical visitor information.

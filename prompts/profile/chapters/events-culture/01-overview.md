## selection_focus
Prefer the homepage and the main "About" / "About us" / "About the festival" / "About the club" page that introduces the organisation or event as a whole.

## synthesis_instructions
Open with an executive summary identifying the subject — festival, theatre, museum, club, venue, or conference — its genre or discipline, its scale, and the audience it serves. In 2-4 paragraphs, convey what it is and what makes it distinctive at a high level, leaving history and mission for the next chapter. Write in neutral, factual prose and avoid promotional hyperbole unless the pages substantiate it. State only what the crawled pages support. Omit the chapter only if no page introduces the subject.

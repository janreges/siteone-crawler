## selection_focus
Prefer "Tickets", "Admission", "Prices", "Box office", "Season tickets", "Subscriptions", "Passes", "Membership", or "Buy tickets" pages describing pricing and purchase.

## synthesis_instructions
Explain how to attend and what it costs: ticket types and price bands, season tickets, subscriptions or festival passes, concessions and discounts, and how and where to buy (box office, online, on-site). Present price categories in a table (ticket type — price — conditions) where the pages provide it and describe the purchase process in short steps. Reproduce prices, categories, and conditions verbatim and never estimate figures the pages omit. Keep the tone factual. Include only what the pages state, and omit the chapter gracefully if the site publishes no ticket or admission information.

## selection_focus
Look for "about", media-kit/"advertise" pages, and any audience or readership descriptions, plus stated circulation, traffic, or demographic figures.

## synthesis_instructions
Characterize who the outlet reaches and how it describes its audience: target readership, demographics, geography, and any stated reach figures (circulation, monthly visitors, subscribers, social following) — media kits are often the richest source. Use short paragraphs plus a bullet list of any concrete metrics, each attributed to its source. Treat self-reported figures as claims. If no audience information is published, keep this brief or omit.

## selection_focus
Look for "about us", "mission", "our values", "editorial guidelines", "code of ethics", "independence", "ownership statement", and corrections/standards pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Describe the outlet's editorial identity: its stated mission and editorial line, claimed values (independence, accuracy, public-interest journalism), and any published standards — ethics code, corrections policy, sourcing rules, or separation of news and opinion. Write in paragraphs. Note where independence or funding transparency is explicitly addressed. Report only what the site states; distinguish concrete published policies from aspirational mission language. Omit gracefully if the outlet publishes no such material.

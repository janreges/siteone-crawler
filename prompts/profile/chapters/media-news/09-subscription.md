## selection_focus

## synthesis_instructions
Explain how the outlet monetizes its audience: paywall type (hard, metered, freemium), subscription and membership tiers with their prices and benefits, print/digital bundles, student/gift options, and any donation or reader-support model. Use a small table or grouped bullet lists for the tiers and terms. State only what the subscription pages describe and quote stated prices as-is. If the outlet is free/ad-only with no reader monetization, note that and omit the tier detail.

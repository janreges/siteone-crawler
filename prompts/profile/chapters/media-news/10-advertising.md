## selection_focus
Look for "advertise", "advertise with us", "media kit", "for advertisers", "partnerships", "branded content", and B2B/commercial pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Summarize the outlet's commercial offer to advertisers and partners: advertising formats and placements, branded/sponsored content and native advertising, newsletter/podcast sponsorship, events, and any stated rates or media-kit contents. Use a bullet list of the distinct commercial products plus a short paragraph on how the outlet positions itself to advertisers. Report only what the commercial pages document. Omit gracefully if the site carries no advertiser-facing information.

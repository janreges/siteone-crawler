## selection_focus
Look for "about", "imprint", "impressum", "company", "publisher", ownership/funding disclosures, and legal/corporate pages.

## synthesis_instructions
Identify who owns and publishes the outlet: the publishing company, parent group or owner, headquarters, history, and any stated funding model (private, subscriber-funded, foundation-backed, public). Write in paragraphs and surface concrete corporate identifiers where given. Base this strictly on disclosed information and separate verifiable ownership facts from PR framing. If ownership is undisclosed, report the verifiable identifiers (imprint/legal entity) or omit.

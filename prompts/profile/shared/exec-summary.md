<task>
The user provides the finished chapters of the profile. Write a SHORT, high-signal executive TL;DR —
a quick orientation, NOT a second overview. A detailed overview chapter follows immediately after
your summary, so do NOT restate it: give the essence in 3-5 tight sentences (roughly 700-1100
characters), naming who the subject is, what they fundamentally offer or do, for whom, and the one or
two things that matter most. You may add a short "**Ve zkratce:**"/"**At a glance:**" line with 3-4
of the most concrete DEFINING facts (e.g. sector / main activity, founding year, scale, headquarters,
ownership, key people) ONLY if those exact facts appear in the chapters. Write clean Markdown without
a top-level heading. Prefer being brief and distinctive over being comprehensive — the chapters carry
the detail.
</task>
<rules>
Offering vs. channel: when you describe what the subject offers or its "main"/"key" offering, name
the actual products or services that ARE its business — the substance customers come for (a bank
offers accounts, loans and investments; a carrier offers shipping; an agency offers its services).
Never present a delivery channel — a mobile or web app, website, portal, platform, e-shop or other
interface — as the subject's product or offering when it is merely the means of ACCESSING that
offering; describe such a tool as a channel, not the offering. (The single exception: when the app or
software itself is the very thing the subject makes and sells — a software product — it IS the
offering.) Prefer describing an offering as a category over forcing a single "product" label onto a
subject that provides a whole portfolio.
Use only what the chapters state — never invent facts, numbers, names, or dates, and never copy a
figure from one entity onto another. Do not repeat whole sentences from the chapters verbatim. Write
in the language '{{language}}'.
</rules>
